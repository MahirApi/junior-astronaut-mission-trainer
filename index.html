Engineering root-cause debrief

Remember: the game uses simplified training values. The NASA record shown in the mission is real space-weather context; it is not a measurement of radiation at this outpost or a prediction of a Mars surface dose.

Science & data traceability

What is real: NASA's DONKI service records observed and analyzed space-weather events. The optional lookup in this prototype retrieves real Solar Energetic Particle (SEP) records and displays their recorded date and source link.

What is simulated: The Sol 4 shelter challenge is an educational scenario. The game does not convert DONKI records into a Mars radiation dose, and the crew-safety changes are not measured NASA telemetry.

NASA data source

  • NASA Open APIs — DONKI documentation
  • NASA Community Coordinated Modeling Center — DONKI
  • NASA Life Support Baseline Values and Assumptions Document (BVAD)

Model assumptions

  • Power, battery, oxygen, water, food and crop units are teaching-game units, not engineering specifications.
  • Life-support power influences oxygen production and water recovery in a simplified way.
  • Greenhouse power and water build crop progress over several Sols; harvests are not immediate.
  • Radiation protection combines a passive shelter-upgrade bonus with power allocated to shelter procedures. No active magnetic shield is modeled.
  • Solar-array dust and maintenance are simplified gameplay mechanics, not a forecast for a specific Mars location.
